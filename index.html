Wargaming Hobby

The wargaming hobby has been around for many, many years easily leading back to the 18th century. It became more popular in the civilian side of life when it was used in the Prussian military by Officer Georg von Reiswitz to help train his solders on battlefield manoeuvres.

This hobby has become a passion for many over the years and spans from the history of old days of soldiers in the bronze age of warfare to the modern, even in fantasy and science fiction. For now, here are my experiences with 3 of them that I collect myself:

  • Warhammer 40000
  • Star Wars legion
  • Starcraft TMG

What you need for building models:

  1. The models from the game system
  2. A pair of clippers or craft knife
  3. Modelling glue for plastic models, superglue for metal models
  4. Model paints... acrylic is preferred for more detailed work
  5. A safe place to keep the models

Warhammer 40,000

Warhammer 40,000 (or Warhammer 40K) has been around since 1987, with a long history of models and immense lore and background to the universe. Not only is it used for wargaming, but the fanbase is also incredible with regular painting competitions for the models they paint and build, to completive tournaments for the game itself.

The premise for Warhammer 40k is a grimdark future where there is only war. They boast around 25-30 different major playable armies in the game. Ranging from the superhuman Space Marines, the savage hordes of Orks looking for a good fight, the mysterious Eldar of ancient days, the nightmarish daemons of Chaos and many, many more.

Star Wars Legion

Star Wars Legion is wargame that focus on the Star Wars universe. It started at March 22, 2018 and has been going strong since then. Taking part from the early stages of the clone wars into the days of the Empire. The time from the movies I – VI.

It’s a has a fantastic collection of models from the movies, going from all the characters you expect in the battles from the mighty Jedi and sinister Sith lords. You can have the Clone Troopers battling against the Syndicate Droid armies. Aswell as the battles of the Republic forces with characters like Luke, Han and Lei leading them against the Empires Storm troopers with Vader, Boba Fett and many more.

Starcraft The Miniature Game

Starcraft The Miniature Game (TMG) just came out with preorders starting on March 10, 2026. This game is a take from a pc game that came out on 31st March 2018. It focuses on a sci-fi universe farm from earth where humanity set up a frontier only to find themselves battling against the overwhelming horde called the Zerg, and an ancient and powerful race called the Protoss.

A collection of models from the game itself are something many have been looking forward to for a long time now. Hope is that the game goes well and we get to see many more to come.
